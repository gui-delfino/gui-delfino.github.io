This course introduces students to dynamics of particles and rigid bodies; chaos and phase space; oscillatory motion, motion under a central force, and scattering off a central potential; non-inertial frames of reference; Lagrangian and Hamiltonian dynamics; coupled oscillations, normal modes, and continuum mechanics. Check out the [course website](https://www.bu.edu/academics/cas/courses/cas-py-408/) for further details.

I'm a discussion teaching assitant, together with Prof. [Michael El-Batanouny](https://www.bu.edu/physics/profile/michael-el-batanouny/). 

All materials for discussion are available on Piazza.
